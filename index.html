How I approach content

Start with a customer question, not a keyword inserted into a sentence.

Separate verified observations from proposed fixes and avoid unsupported ranking or revenue claims.

Write a useful answer; map it to the relevant page and a clear next action.

Measure impressions and clicks separately from inquiries and completed sales.
