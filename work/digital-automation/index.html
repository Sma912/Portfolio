DIGITAL PRODUCT · BUSINESS AUTOMATION

Revivio & operational automation

Practical digital systems rooted in lead tracking, source identification and information handling.

Revivio — service-business pages

A dedicated landing-page concept for small service businesses showing services, prices, photos, hours and contact options. Incoming paths can be source-labeled before opening WhatsApp; this is contact attribution, not proof that the page generates customers.

European Lead Finder

A web application to retrieve business information through Google Places API, maintain deduplicated lead records, draft localized outreach and track statuses. Built with JavaScript, Netlify Functions, GitHub, spreadsheets and AI-assisted editing.

Relevant editorial decisions

For each outreach message, start with verified business details, adapt the offer to the business and have a human approve the draft. No fabricated reviews, missing-website claims or speculative audience metrics.

  • Google Places API and Netlify Functions
  • Google Sheets, source labels and lead states
  • Human-reviewed multilingual outreach
