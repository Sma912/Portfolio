Revivio: business information and contact flows

Revivio is a service-business page and contact-flow concept. The aim is to help people who discover a business through Google, Instagram, campaigns, or QR codes find useful service details and start a WhatsApp conversation.

A dedicated page can present services, prices, photos, opening hours, and location. Source-labeled paths help distinguish where contact interest began. This should not be confused with automatically generating customers or proving sales attribution.

European Lead Finder

A personal workflow for researching European service businesses through Google Places data, organizing leads, drafting language-appropriate outreach, adding Persian review translations, and tracking follow-up stages.

Tools and workflows include Google Places API, JavaScript, Netlify Functions, JSON, spreadsheets, and AI-assisted editorial review. The goal is verifiable lead research, not bulk claims about outreach performance.
