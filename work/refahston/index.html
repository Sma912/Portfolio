Refahston: retail, customer journeys, and digital operations

An e-commerce and installment-sales business used as a real-world context for customer communication, process organization and SEO research.

Business background

Refahston is a retail and installment-sales business in consumer electronics, digital products and home appliances. My role covered business operations, customer processes, product information, online workflows and coordination among sales and technical activities.

Customer and content challenges

People considering an installment purchase often need clear information about eligibility, down payments, payment schedules, warranty and delivery. Accurate and comprehensible content is part of their buying journey, alongside the product itself.

This business perspective informs my approach to web content: answer the user’s real question before asking them to take the next step.

Relevant platform and process experience

  • Product and category information for digital retail.
  • WordPress / WooCommerce workflows and customer journey planning.
  • Excel, database-supported reporting, CRM and automation.
  • SEO research covering search intent, page structure, title tags and external link data.
