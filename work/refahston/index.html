Refahston: where e-commerce content meets search intent

A live consumer-electronics store provides a real framework for explaining product choices, payments, category navigation and the SEO work worth prioritizing.

The business

Founded in 2019, Refahston combined consumer-electronics retail, installment sales and an agent network. My responsibilities involved understanding product information, purchase terms, customer communication and operations.

The web work

WordPress/WooCommerce belonged to the earlier online retail workflow, alongside CRM and custom systems. This background is relevant because content quality affects how buyers compare products and understand financing and availability.

From business work to SEO briefs

The companion SEO study converts those real customer questions into page intent, content gaps, a suggested category hierarchy, structured product information and a measurement plan. It clearly separates proposed SEO actions from verified implementations.
