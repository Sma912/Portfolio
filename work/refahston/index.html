Refahston: online retail and customer journeys

Operating a digital retail business taught me how customers compare products, ask about installment terms, and look for trustworthy purchase information.

What I worked on

  • Product and category information for digital devices and home appliances; communications around installment eligibility and purchasing steps.
  • WordPress/WooCommerce store workflows, CRM/reporting processes and coordination between online operations, customers and technical implementation.
  • Using questions heard in real customer journeys to identify content opportunities: how a product differs from alternatives, what the total cost is and what happens after purchase.

What this experience contributed to SEO thinking

  • A product page must answer transactional intent; a comparison article answers a different stage of the same customer journey.
  • Accurate product details, clear payment explanations and straightforward navigation help users before any optimization claims are made.
  • The 2026 SEO research builds on these years of e-commerce work, rather than retrospectively treating all retail operations as a specialized SEO campaign.
