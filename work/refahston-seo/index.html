Refahston: e-commerce experience and a practical SEO roadmap

A real retail business is the starting point. This case separates the online-store work I have done since 2019 from SEO research undertaken in 2026 and the optimizations that remain proposals.

A real retail business is the starting point. This case separates the online-store work I have done since 2019 from SEO research undertaken in 2026 and the optimizations that remain proposals.

Business context — documented experience since 2019

  • Refahston operated in consumer electronics, household products and installment-based retail. Its online presence used WordPress and WooCommerce alongside customer and sales workflows. My responsibilities were centered on business operations, product information, sales processes and customer-facing communication.
  • Common buyer questions included product differences, prices, availability, installment terms, warranty and delivery. They provide a grounded starting point for useful search-oriented content.

SEO research and review — 2026

  • Mapped informational, comparison and transactional search intent to suitable page types, using examples such as Samsung phones in Qazvin.
  • Reviewed SEO titles, H1 headings, meta descriptions and the relationship between product pages, category pages and educational guides.
  • Studied off-page SEO and backlink interpretation, including referring domains and link attributes; third-party tool numbers are not presented as verified traffic or ranking gains.
  • Prepared a 7-part, 28-chapter plan for a Persian textbook on data-driven off-page SEO and link building; the initial lessons are drafted and the manuscript remains in progress.

Recommended WooCommerce SEO implementation plan — not claimed as completed

  • 1. Technical baseline: verify crawl access, indexing status, canonical URLs, XML sitemap, mobile experience and important templates.
  • 2. Search-intent map: allocate category, product, comparison, financing-information and local pages to distinct user needs; avoid duplicating pages for every keyword variation.
  • 3. Content: rewrite thin product descriptions and FAQs using actual specifications, costs, return policies and payment terms; use unique titles, headings and useful alt text.
  • 4. Internal links: connect buying guides and categories to relevant products with descriptive anchor text.
  • 5. Structured data: validate eligible Product, Breadcrumb and Organization properties against Google documentation; never invent ratings or availability.
  • 6. Local visibility: verify accurate business contact details, location and relevant Google Business Profile information.
  • 7. Measurement: record a baseline in Search Console and GA4 if available; evaluate impressions, clicks, relevant engagement and confirmed business outcomes separately.
  • 8. Off-page work: earn editorial links through useful guides and partnerships; avoid buying links for ranking manipulation.

Evidence and limitations

  • The 2019 start date describes business/e-commerce involvement, not seven years of dedicated SEO-specialist work.
  • No verified before/after ranking, organic traffic, conversion lift or backlink campaign result is claimed here.
  • The implementation roadmap is a proposed professional approach and requires access to live analytics and website administration to validate.
