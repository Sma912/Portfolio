Refahston: a business-led approach to SEO

A case study linking years of e-commerce and product content work with a transparent, current SEO assessment—not rewriting the history as a long-running agency SEO campaign.

View live Refahston website ↗
Public-page observations · 23 October 2026 · Proof of work, not a claim of rank improvements

01 · The actual business context

Refahston began as a retail and installment-sales business in 2019. Its work included product catalogues, customer inquiries, explanations about payment options and web operations. WordPress/WooCommerce formed part of the earlier online-store experience; the currently visible public site uses its own current interface. That operational history is the basis for the content work—it is not proof of seven years of specialist SEO campaigns.

02 · What the live website shows

The public Refahston website presently lists mobile phones, tablets, laptops, accessories and other device categories. Product pages provide model, memory, color, price and stock confirmation information. The purchase terms explain that stock and final payment information are confirmed before payment. These are directly observable topics on which useful commercial content can be built.

03 · Translate the buying journey into search intent

Informational intent: explain payment requirements, registration, device variants and delivery. Commercial research: compare models and storage options, clarify who each product is suitable for. Transactional intent: deliver clear category and product pages with accurate stock, price and purchasing steps. Local intent: where relevant, include accurate geographical and service information. Each page should answer a distinct question rather than repeat generic keywords.

04 · A specific product-page example

A Samsung Galaxy A17 page on Refahston displays a 128GB/4GB configuration, colors and pricing. A search-oriented content brief would retain the exact model and variant in the title and H1, present specifications in readable sections, answer payment and delivery questions only when current policies support them, and link to the relevant Samsung category. This is an editorial proposal, not a claim that these changes have been implemented.

05 · Technical and measurement priorities

Audit indexability, canonical URLs and duplicate product variants; verify rendered text, metadata, internal links, structured Product data and Core Web Vitals. Use Search Console for query impressions and click-through trends, and analytics for engaged visits and conversions if instrumentation is available. Examine links by referring domain and context, not by backlink count alone. Prioritize by user impact, implementation effort and evidence.

06 · Why a hiring team may care

This work joins firsthand retail operations with repeatable editorial decision-making. I can interview stakeholders, clarify the real customer question, write or edit explanatory copy, prepare a page-level content brief and document what still requires verification. It does not claim that proposed technical changes were deployed or that rankings improved.
