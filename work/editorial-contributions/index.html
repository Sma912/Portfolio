Selected editorial contributions

Small assignments matter when they reveal the actual scope: product descriptions, short web copy and practical website advice.

Scope: submitted short content; no public URL or publication proof attached.

Consumer-electronics content for a large marketplace

I prepared several short product-related content items for Digikala in the digital-products area, including two micro-articles of approximately 300 characters each. This was a limited editorial contribution, not a staff position, a multi-year content contract or evidence of SEO performance.

Small website content and SEO guidance

I also supported several smaller websites with content and practical advice on making their pages work better for visitors and search. The clients are not named publicly. This experience is positioned as selected project work rather than an invented agency case study.

How I work with a content brief

Start with the exact question and target reader; confirm product/service facts; assign one clear page purpose; prepare headings, concise explanatory copy and a direct CTA; review language, accuracy and useful internal links. Track published changes and measurement separately from drafting.
