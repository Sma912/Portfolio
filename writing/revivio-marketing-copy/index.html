Revivio: writing a clear value proposition

Landing-page and outreach copy explaining a direct WhatsApp contact path for small service businesses—without implying it automatically creates new customers.

Download PDF ↗

One clear page. Easier conversations.

  • Customers may discover a salon on Google, see photos on Instagram or scan a QR code at the shop. They still need the same basics: available services, indicative prices, opening hours and a way to ask a question.
  • Revivio is a simple dedicated-page and contact-flow concept: put practical information in one place and let people continue the conversation through familiar WhatsApp.

How the customer journey is designed

  • Present clear services, prices, photos, opening hours and location.
  • Offer direct WhatsApp paths from Google, Instagram or QR links with a label that identifies the originating channel.
  • Give the business owner a view of available source information without confusing a contact click with a completed sale.
  • Make it easier to share a Google review link or QR code with customers.

A transparent offer

  • Try a dedicated page for 30 days, free and without commitment, with your real customers.
  • The page does not generate customers on its own. It aims to make information and contact simpler for people who already discover the business.

Short outreach sample

  • Hi [First Name], I found [Business Name] while looking at [service] businesses in [City]. [Add one real, verified detail.] I am working on a simple page that gives customers one place to find your services, prices, photos and opening hours. Links from Google and Instagram can lead into WhatsApp with a source label. If useful, I can prepare a page for a free 30-day test with no commitment. Best, Morteza.

Portfolio note

  • This is sample commercial copy for a developing product. It is not a statement of completed client projects, tested conversion uplift or guaranteed results.

Complete English marketing copy

One clear business page. Direct conversations. Better source visibility.

Revivio is a simple business-page and contact-flow concept for local service providers. Your page presents the essentials—services, prices, photos, opening hours, and location—while source-labeled links from your different channels can guide interested people into WhatsApp.

Primary CTA: Try your dedicated page free for 30 days.

No commitment. Test the idea with the customers who already find your business.

The problem is not always a lack of attention

Someone discovers your salon on Google. Another person sees your work on Instagram. A third scans a QR code in your shop. Each person has a question, but the next step is not always clear: What does this service cost? When are you open? How do they reach you?

A customer should not need to piece together basic answers before sending a message.

How Revivio is designed to work

  1. Show what matters. Give people a dedicated page with your services, prices, photos, location, opening hours, and a clear WhatsApp contact option.
  2. Make contact easy. Use direct WhatsApp paths from Google, Instagram, QR codes, or campaigns, with source labels that help identify where an interaction began.
  3. Keep the conversation familiar. Continue answering customers in WhatsApp. Revivio does not ask you to replace the way you already speak with them.
  4. Learn which paths people use. Review available source information inside the business dashboard to distinguish visits and contact actions from confirmed sales.
  5. Make reviews easier to request. Offer customers a simple QR path to your Google review page, rather than asking them to search for your listing.

Built around your business, not a complicated new routine

A salon owner needs different information from a repair service or a private tutor. The page can be organized around what your customers ask most often—without an oversized website or another inbox for every channel.

What Revivio does not promise

A dedicated page does not bring new customers by itself. It helps people who already discover your business find the information they need and take a clearer next step. Source labels show where interest begins; they do not prove a sale without separate conversion tracking.

Try it with real customers

We can prepare a dedicated page for your business to test for 30 days, free and without commitment. Use it alongside your existing Google, Instagram, and WhatsApp presence. See whether it makes customer questions and contact paths simpler in everyday work.

Final CTA: Start a free 30-day trial.

Short outreach email — an example for a verified lead

Subject: A small idea for [Business Name]

Hi [First Name],

I came across [Business Name] while looking at [service type] businesses in [City]. [Insert one genuine, verified observation about the business.]

I’m working on Revivio, a simple dedicated page where customers can find your services, prices, photos, and opening hours. Links from Google, Instagram, or a QR code can lead people to your WhatsApp with a source label, so you can better understand where an inquiry began.

It won’t create customers on its own. The idea is to make it easier for people who already find you to get the details they need and contact you.

If it sounds useful, I can prepare a page for you to test for 30 days, free and without commitment, with your real customers.

Best,
Morteza
Revivio

Editing note: Replace the bracketed fields with observed facts before sending; do not fabricate praise, a website gap, or a customer problem.
